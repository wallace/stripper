# Link Stripper Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 8 October 2026_

Link Stripper does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

**What the app does on your Mac**

- When you copy something, Link Stripper checks whether the clipboard holds a single web
  link. On macOS 15.4 and later this check is done by macOS without the app reading the
  clipboard contents.
- If it is a web link, Link Stripper reads it, removes known tracking parameters, and
  puts the cleaned link back on the clipboard. Anything else you copy is left alone.
- Clipboard content marked as concealed or transient (for example, by password managers)
  is never touched.
- The app remembers the last original link only in memory, so you can restore it from
  the menu. It is gone when the app quits.
- Your settings (on/off and notifications) are stored locally by macOS.

**What the app does not do**

- It makes no network connections, so nothing you copy ever leaves your Mac.
- It includes no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs.
- It does not create accounts or ask for any personal information.
